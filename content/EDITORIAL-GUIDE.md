# Editorial guide for Insights

The Insights hub publishes what Gibson chooses to publish. Nothing is generated or posted automatically.

## Voice
- Plain British English. Short sentences. Say the thing.
- Write for a reader who is busy and capable, not an expert in your field.
- First person is fine. Opinion is fine, labelled as such: "I think", "in my experience", "the evidence says".

## Three kinds of statement, kept apart
1. **Experience**: what you did and saw. Say so. "At Argent we found..."
2. **Opinion**: what you think follows. Say so. "My view is..."
3. **Fact**: a claim about the world. Needs a source in the Sources list, and the date you checked it. Figures, laws, prices and product features change; verify anything time-sensitive on the day you publish and record the date in `updated` if you revise it later.

## Sources
- Link to the original: the report, the law, the vendor page, not a summary of it.
- Prefer official and primary sources: gov.uk, ICO, NCSC, the publisher's own site.
- No source, no claim.

## Original work and licences
- Write it yourself. Quote others briefly and attribute them.
- Images: your own, or licensed for reuse with the licence recorded in the article's cover field or Sources. No screenshots of other people's content without permission.
- Music and audio: credit contributors; keep licence records as in CREDITS.md.

## What never goes in
- Claims about qualifications or results that are not true and verifiable.
- Identifiable information about learners, families, clients or colleagues.
- Anything that reads as marketing dressed as advice.

## SEND content
- Say "Special Educational Needs and Disabilities" the first time, then SEND.
- Person-first or identity-first language as the people concerned prefer; when unsure, ask or use both.
- No real or composite case studies that could identify a young person. General patterns only.
- Do not imply clinical, legal or safeguarding authority beyond your actual role and qualifications.

## Cybersecurity content
- Written for small-business owners and families, not practitioners. Defensive guidance only.
- Explain what a recommendation protects against and where it stops. No exploit detail, no step-by-step attack narratives.
- Point readers to the NCSC and the ICO for authoritative guidance and to a professional for a live incident.

## Music and creativity
- Credit co-writers, performers and producers. Record licences for any sample or stock element.
- Reuse of your music by others goes through you; do not imply blanket permission.

## Workflow
1. Write on a branch, not on main (anything on main is public).
2. Fill every required field in the template. Run the article locally and read it on a phone.
3. Check sources and dates. Set `status: "published"`, merge, push. Amplify deploys.
4. Add the article URL to sitemap.xml.
