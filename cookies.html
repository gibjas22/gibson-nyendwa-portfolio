Cookies and similar technologies

Audited 27 October 2026, updated 29 October 2026 for the appearance setting and self-hosted fonts, by inspecting the site's own code and the requests a browser makes when loading it.

The short version

This site sets no cookies of its own and does not use session storage or any analytics or advertising service. It keeps one setting in your browser's local storage: the colour theme you choose under Appearance. There is no consent banner because nothing optional runs on page load.

Cookie settings

No optional technology runs until you ask for it. The controls that exist are the buttons described below: nothing from a third party loads before you press them.

Your appearance setting

When you pick Purple, Sky Blue, White or Automatic, or close the theme chooser, the site saves that one word in your browser's local storage under the name gn-appearance, so the next page and your next visit use the same theme. It holds nothing else, is never sent to this site or anyone else, and stays on this browser only. To go back to the rotating theme, choose Automatic under Appearance. To remove the entry entirely, clear this site's data in your browser settings. If your browser blocks storage, the theme still works for the page you are on.

Observed versus documented

"None observed" means a browser loading this site showed no cookies or storage set by that provider at the time of the audit. What a third party does after you choose to load its content is set out in its own documentation, linked from its service, and may change.

If this changes

If analytics or any other optional technology is ever added, this page will be updated and a consent control will be added before it runs, with rejecting as easy as accepting.
