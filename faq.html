Digital Marketing

Being found: search, Google Business Profile, websites that convert, and the local angle for West Midlands businesses. The same subjects Gibcom works on every week.

SEO (search engine optimisation) earns visibility by making your site the best answer to what people search for; it is slower to build and keeps working when you stop paying attention. Paid advertising buys visibility for as long as the budget runs; it is immediate and stops the day you stop. Most small businesses need some of each, in a proportion that depends on margins and how quickly they need results.

Book a 30-minute call or send a message from the Contact section. Say what you are trying to achieve and what you have already tried; that makes the first conversation useful.

Cybersecurity

Owners of small businesses, and families, who need to protect themselves without a security team. The guidance is defensive and written in plain language; it is not for practitioners and it never includes attack detail.

No. If you think you are being attacked or have been breached, act now: disconnect the affected device, change passwords from a different device, and contact a professional. The National Cyber Security Centre publishes guidance for small businesses and individuals, and Action Fraud takes reports in the UK. Articles here explain principles; they cannot assess your situation.

Email info@gcmarketingsupport.co with "Security" in the subject line and a description of what you found. I read everything that comes in. There is no formal disclosure programme or reward scheme on this site.

SEND & Inclusive Education

Special Educational Needs and Disabilities. In England it describes a child or young person who has a learning difficulty or disability that calls for special educational provision. The framework is set out in the SEND Code of Practice, published by the Department for Education.

Practical perspectives from twenty years in specialist and college settings: craft-based learning, transition to adult life, and what helps young people do the things they were told they could not. Content stays at the level of general patterns; nothing identifies a learner or family.

No. Every young person's needs and legal entitlements are specific. For decisions about a particular child, speak to their school or college SENCO, the local authority SEND team, or an independent adviser such as your local SENDIASS service.

Contact, Booking and Privacy

Press Book a call anywhere on the site. It opens my iMeetify scheduling page, where you choose a 30-minute online slot in your own time zone. You can also load the calendar on the Contact section itself.

Your message is emailed to me and I reply from my own inbox. It is not added to any list and nobody else sees it. I aim to reply within two working days, but that is an aim rather than a promise.

Only to reply to you, or to set up the call you booked. The privacy notice sets out what is collected, who processes it (AWS for the form, iMeetify for bookings) and your rights.

This site sets no cookies. The only setting it keeps is the colour theme you choose under Appearance, saved in your own browser so it carries across pages and visits. You can change it there at any time, or choose Automatic to follow the fortnightly rotation. The cookies page explains what the site does and does not do, including the embeds that only load when you press a button.
