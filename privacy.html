Privacy notice

Last substantive update: 27 October 2026.

Who operates this site

This personal portfolio is operated by Gibson Nyendwa, an individual based in Birmingham, UK, who is the controller for any personal information collected through it. It is not operated by Gibcom Marketing Support Ltd. Contact for anything in this notice: info@gcmarketingsupport.co.

What is collected, and why

Nothing, unless you choose to send it. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking technologies on this site. Information reaches me in three ways:

  • The contact form or an email. Your name, email address, message, and if you add them, a telephone number and the kind of enquiry. Used only to reply to you. My lawful basis is legitimate interests: responding to someone who has written to me, which is what you would expect when you write.
  • Booking a call. Handled by iMeetify. It collects what it needs to create the appointment (name, email, the slot you pick) under its own privacy terms, shown on its booking page. I receive the booking details so the call can happen.
  • Visiting the site. The hosting provider keeps standard server logs (IP address, pages requested, browser type) for operating and securing the service. I do not use those logs to identify visitors.

Who processes it

  • Amazon Web Services, Europe (London) region: hosts the site (Amplify, CloudFront, S3) and, when the form is live, carries form messages to my inbox (API Gateway, Lambda, Simple Email Service).
  • iMeetify: bookings.
  • Media providers (YouTube, Vimeo, Spotify, Apple, SoundCloud): only if you choose to load or follow a link to their content.

Messages you send are not shared with anyone else and are not added to any mailing list.

Where it is held

The site and the form run in AWS's London region. Email is read in my ordinary mailbox. Providers such as iMeetify and the media platforms may process data outside the UK under their own arrangements; their privacy notices describe the safeguards they rely on.

How long it is kept

Enquiries are kept in my mailbox for as long as needed to deal with them and any follow-up, and are then deleted. The form itself stores nothing: your message exists only as the email it sends. Booking records are kept by iMeetify under its own retention terms.

Your rights

You can ask what I hold about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, object to its use, or ask me to stop contacting you, by emailing info@gcmarketingsupport.co. If you are unhappy with how I handle your information you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Cookies

This site sets none. It stores one setting in your own browser, the colour theme you choose under Appearance, which never leaves your device. The cookies page lists exactly what loads and when.
