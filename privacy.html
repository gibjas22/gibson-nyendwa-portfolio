Privacy notice

This site: gibson-nyendwa-portfolio, run by Gibson Nyendwa, Birmingham, UK. Last updated 27 October 2026.

What this site collects

Nothing, unless you choose to send it. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies on this site. The only information I receive is what you type into the contact form, send by email, or enter when you book a call.

The contact form

The form asks for your name, email address and message, and optionally a telephone number and the kind of enquiry. I use this only to reply to you. It is delivered to my inbox, info@gcmarketingsupport.co, using Amazon Web Services (API Gateway, Lambda and Simple Email Service) in the Europe (London) region. Nothing you send is added to a mailing list or shared with anyone else. The form does not store your message anywhere other than the email it sends.

Booking a call

Appointments are handled by iMeetify. If you book, iMeetify collects the details it needs to create the appointment (name, email and the slot you pick) under its own privacy terms, which it shows on the booking page. The calendar only loads on this site when you ask it to, by pressing "Show calendar here"; the "Book a call" button opens iMeetify in a new tab instead.

Audio on the mixing desk

The audio loops play from this site's own files and send no data anywhere. Nothing starts until you move a fader.

Links to other sites

Links to LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube and GitHub take you to those services, which have their own privacy notices.

Your rights

You can ask me what I hold about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or ask me to stop contacting you, by emailing info@gcmarketingsupport.co. Under UK data protection law you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Hosting

The site is hosted on AWS Amplify, which keeps standard web server logs (such as IP addresses and pages requested) for operating the service. I do not use those logs to identify visitors.
